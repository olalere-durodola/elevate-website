04 / How it works

Three years, three jobs

5th – 6th grade

Develop

Footwork, both hands, and a shot that survives contact. Everyone plays real minutes. We do not run a bench at this level, and we do not chase trophies with a six-man rotation.

Explore development→
7th – 8th grade

Compete

Tournament schedule, scouting reports, and film every week. Minutes are earned here, and players learn to handle that before high school does it to them.

Explore competition→
JV – Varsity

Advance

Strength work, position-specific coaching, and honest conversations about where each player fits at the next level. We prepare for the school program, not around it.
